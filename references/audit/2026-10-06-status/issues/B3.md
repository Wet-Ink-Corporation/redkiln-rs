TITLE: Stop identity::read swallowing probe errors as "no identity" (AC-179 bypass)
LABELS: bug
STATUS: untracked

## Finding
`identity::read` probes `sqlite_master` with `.unwrap_or(false)`, so any error (busy, locked, corrupt or not-a-database file, I/O) is read as "the identity table does not exist" and returns `Ok(None)`. Its own doc says it errors "on any failure other than an absent table or row".

## Evidence
- crates/rk-store/src/identity.rs:63-74 @ ce4ed1a: `conn.query_row("SELECT 1 FROM sqlite_master ...", [], |_| Ok(true)).unwrap_or(false)`; the doc at :65-66 promises `StoreError::Sqlite` for other failures.
- crates/rk-store/src/migrate.rs:50-60 @ ce4ed1a: `establish` treats `None` as a fresh store: it runs `apply(conn)` and `initialise` (INSERT OR IGNORE) instead of the AC-179 foreign-store refusal.
- Unchanged at 62dd3b8. No record at ce4ed1a (runbook/, spikes/DECISIONS-*.md, references/adr) mentions it.

## Impact
A foreign or other-version store whose probe fails transiently (SQLITE_BUSY, a lock, a read error) can skip the identity check and be migrated, while `INSERT OR IGNORE` leaves the old stamp in place. That is the outcome AC-179/AC-180 forbid. The window is narrow (the call runs inside one write transaction), but the contract is violated by construction.

## Suggested fix
Distinguish "no row" from "error": use `query_row(...).optional()` (rusqlite `OptionalExtension`) and propagate other errors with `?`. Add tests that a connection whose probe fails (for example a non-database file, or a locked connection) returns `Err(StoreError::Sqlite)` from `read` and from `establish`, not `Ok(None)`.

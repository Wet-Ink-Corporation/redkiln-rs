LABELS: documentation, enhancement
STATUS: untracked

## Finding
Documentation and comment rot, plus two god modules. Dated provenance comments are 25-50% of source lines, about 200 "proposed" labels remain for ADRs now accepted, and some crate and module headers contradict the code. rk-cli verbs/run.rs and rk-git worktree.rs are very large.

## Evidence
- Sizes @ ce4ed1a: crates/rk-cli/src/verbs/run.rs = 6,481 lines; crates/rk-git/src/worktree.rs = 2,883 lines.
- Contradicting headers: crates/rk-cli/src/lib.rs:23 ("doctor is a SKELETON ... RULES is empty"); doctor/mod.rs:4, :16; doctor/rules.rs:19-22; verbs/answer.rs:7, :283 ("THIS FILE IS A SKELETON"); verbs/doctor.rs:37 ("CYCLE 2: RULES is empty"). crates/rk-kernel/src/lib.rs:1-25 describes Step 7b as slices 1-2 with steps 5-7 "not built", later partly corrected inline.
- "proposed": 241 .rs files under crates/ match the word; many are ADRs since accepted (e.g. ADR-0191 accepted on D21-131 per s8g:1053).
- git grep at ce4ed1a of PROGRESS.md and s8-exit-criterion.md for post-M5 hardening or splitting run.rs finds no scheduled item.

## Impact
Readers and checkpoint greps (which assert on prose) rely on misleading text; modules of this size resist review and every touch triggers whole-checkpoint re-runs.

## Suggested action
After M5 (not before; edits re-trigger checkpoints): (1) mechanical sweep of "proposed" labels against ADR status; (2) fix or delete contradicting headers; (3) move dated provenance into the ledgers; (4) split run.rs and worktree.rs behind existing tests; (5) cap stage-file size for Phase 2. Track as one milestone with sub-issues.

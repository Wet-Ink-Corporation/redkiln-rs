## Finding
Step 8j's entry gate is Checkpoint 8i (S-BIN). No record justifies the serial gate. 8j's build work (human roots, hold_before, lane_proof, shortlist, amendments, CI reconcile) could run alongside S-BIN B2/B3, while paid steps and the pin move stay behind 8i.

## Evidence
- runbook/INDEX.md:87 @ ce4ed1a: row 29 Step 8j, entry gate "Checkpoint 8i". runbook/phase-1/s8j-run-preparation.md:62 only restates "The entry gate stays Checkpoint 8i" (D21-46 revision) with no reason; no overlap or parallel-work statement in s8j or s8i (git grep at ce4ed1a).
- ADR-0093 decision 4 and ADR-0165 say S-BIN does not block Phase-1 work (appendix §7 GAP4). 8j consumes one S-BIN output, the pinned cli_version, already known (2.1.289).
- Hard constraint: 8i's Rust package (rk-ports, rk-store, rk-cli, possibly rk-kernel) must land before 8j.6 step 1b's proof re-recording.

## Impact
Roughly 1-2 days saved by the audit's estimate (more if 8j is as large as the 7.5 d recomputation), capped by the single operator.

## Suggested action
Options: (a) re-point to 8h, with conditions: the 8j chain still re-runs 8i; 8j.6 step 1b and all paid steps stay behind 8i and its Rust package; each push gets its own go-ahead; pin target 2.1.289; edit INDEX.md:87 and the s8j header, and add a ledger entry; (b) keep serial, and record the reason; (c) split 8j into 8j-build (gate 8h) and 8j-run (gate 8i). Recommend (c) or (a); (a) is the smaller edit.

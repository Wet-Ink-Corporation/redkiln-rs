TITLE: Wire budget exhaustion gate and degradation policy into the run path
LABELS: bug, enhancement
STATUS: untracked

## Finding
The budget-exhaustion machinery (the AC-024 wall-clock mark writer, the AC-025 exhaustion gate) and the model-degradation policy are implemented and unit-tested but called by nothing under `crates/*/src`. Per-pass attempt charges are journaled, but no code folds them into a ledger or closes a gate on exhaustion. `substitute` is never used for a dispatch.

## Evidence
- crates/rk-kernel/src/workflow/budget.rs:873 `evaluate_gate`, :816 `exhausted_counter`, :1050 `wall_clock_expiry_mark` @ ce4ed1a: git grep finds no caller outside budget.rs, its tests and doc comments.
- crates/rk-kernel/src/workflow/degrade.rs:285 `substitute`, :320 `degraded_event`, :388 `gate_closes`: no caller outside tests/it/degradation.rs.
- crates/rk-kernel/src/runtime.rs:93 marks AC-024 "ARMED, NOT DISCHARGED ... ZERO call sites". budget.rs:~810 repeats it.
- Only the charge helpers are live: runtime/step.rs:1708 (`charge_dispatched`), :2186 (`charge_for_each`).
- No record in s8j-run-preparation.md, s8-exit-criterion.md, PROGRESS.md or DECISIONS schedules the wiring; DECISIONS-2026-09-14.md D-8 routed only the re-exec test harness.

## Impact
The declared `budget` (attempts, tokens, wall clock) on activities is not enforced as a gate at run time. The per-item dispatch cap and token appetite (workflow/envelope.rs, s8h) is the only live bound. AC-024, AC-025 and the degradation ACs are not evidenced on a real path.

## Suggested fix
In the step executor, fold `pass_charges()` into a `BudgetLedger` after each pass and call `evaluate_gate`/`exhausted_counter` before the next dispatch. Write `wall_clock_expiry_mark` on expiry and route `substitute` through the harness dispatch. Or record an ADR that defers both to Phase 2 and amend the ACs.

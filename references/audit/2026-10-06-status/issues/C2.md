TITLE: Add a landing step to the runbook: --no-ff merge of build/phase-0-to-1 to main at M5
LABELS: enhancement, documentation
STATUS: untracked

## Finding
Nothing in the runbook plans how build/phase-0-to-1 (419 commits past main at 353ea01) reaches main. A squash or rebase would orphan the 189+ branch-unique commit hashes cited in the records.

## Evidence
- runbook/INDEX.md:95-100 @ ce4ed1a: the M5 row ("Phase 1 exits", reached when Checkpoint 8 passed) has no landing or merge action. INDEX.md, PROGRESS.md, 00-preamble.md, halts.md and s8-exit-criterion.md contain no no-ff or merge-to-main step (git grep at ce4ed1a). The "landing" in s8f is the kernel's landed commit on rk-run refs, a different concept.
- Only precedent: the --no-ff merge 66d0ced (remediation branch). git rev-list --count 353ea01..ce4ed1a = 419. main holds no code (no Cargo.toml).

## Impact
An ad hoc squash or rebase at M5 would break the evidence chain (hashes cited from PROGRESS, ADRs and ledgers).

## Suggested action
Options: (a) add an M5 landing step: --no-ff merge to main after checkpoints and CI are green; (b) fast-forward only (keeps hashes, loses the merge marker); (c) squash or rebase (rejected: orphans hashes). Recommend (a). In the same step untrack the four stale spikes/*.log files, and fix the order: merge the remote (C1), re-run checkpoints, get CI green, then land.

## Finding
The Capability/Probe/Row registry module is copied in four adapter crates, and run.rs/env.rs are near-duplicates between the Rust and Node toolchain adapters. The registry copying is a recorded, deliberate choice (RT-24) with the shared home still owed. The run.rs/env.rs duplication is partly stated in code but not scheduled.

## Evidence
- crates/rk-adapter-{claude,sqlite,toolchain-rust,toolchain-node}/src/registry.rs @ ce4ed1a: 147, 239, 283 and 218 lines; each declares its own `Row`, `Probe`, `Capability` (e.g. rk-adapter-claude registry.rs:46,77,98). registry.rs:5-14 states the four `Capability` bodies are byte-equal by design and Checkpoint 7c digests them.
- runbook/phase-1/s7c-adapters.md:168 (one shape, declared in each crate) and :3210 ("RT-24 the capability vocabulary's home (four byte-equal copies until then)") own the deferral. ADR-0135:21 and ADR-0170:368 rely on the byte-equal bodies.
- crates/rk-adapter-toolchain-rust/src/run.rs (232 lines) vs rk-adapter-toolchain-node/src/run.rs (217): same `run`/`run_in` shape, about 67 differing lines (mostly docs). env.rs 110 vs 120 lines; rk-adapter-toolchain-node/src/env.rs:14-19 restates the denylist "rather than imported" and admits nothing checks the lists against each other.
- Unchanged at 62dd3b8.

## Impact
Maintenance debt: a fix to run-bounded handling, env capture or the vocabulary must be made in up to four places; only the Capability body is digest-checked, the denylist and run plumbing are not.

## Suggested fix
When RT-24's home is decided, move `Row`/`Probe`/`Capability` and the loader into one crate (rk-ports or a small rk-adapter-support) and delete the digest check; extract `run_in` and the env capture there as well, or add a CI check that the two denylists and the run bodies stay equal. No behaviour change; existing conformance tests are the guard.

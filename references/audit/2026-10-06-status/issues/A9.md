TITLE: Wire review-gate quarantine, advisory retirement and AC-349 binding into runs
LABELS: enhancement
STATUS: untracked

## Finding
The real re-executor (`ToolchainReExecutor`) reproduces only `kind = run` findings; `mutate` returns `None`, which is read as "unverifiable". Of the review-gate library functions, `quarantine::grant`/`remove`, `confirm::retire_not_re_raised` and (for AC-349) `verify::claims::bind` have no caller on the run path. `claims::bind` runs in `doctor` only.

## Evidence
- crates/rk-cli/src/review_pass.rs:196-222 @ ce4ed1a (= 62dd3b8): `if finding.repro.kind != ReproKind::Run { return None; }`. The doc at :208-218 says `mutate` is refused by name.
- crates/rk-kernel/src/review/quarantine.rs:50 `grant` and :80 `remove`: no caller under crates/*/src (only the event-kind tables and doctor read the rows).
- crates/rk-kernel/src/review/confirm.rs:351 `retire_not_re_raised`: no caller. runtime/advisory.rs:52 says the lifecycle is "not built".
- crates/rk-cli/src/doctor/rules.rs:2432 is the only caller of `claims::bind`.
- Records: AC-349's plan-time binding is listed as owed with no owner at runbook/phase-1/s8g-verify-and-restored-demands.md:2380 ("No sub-stage owns wiring the binder") and spikes/DECISIONS-2026-09-21.md:5770. The mutate refusal is ADR-0162. Quarantine and retirement appear in no owed list.

## Impact
No run can grant or lift a quarantine or retire an advisory, so those behaviours exist only as library tests and doctor checks. A toolchain-claim tie surfaces at doctor time, not at plan time.

## Suggested fix
Split into three small items. (1) Call `claims::bind` in the plan step and refuse a tie by name. (2) Call `retire_not_re_raised` after each review pass. (3) Add a verb for quarantine grant/remove, or record in an ADR that they are Phase 2.

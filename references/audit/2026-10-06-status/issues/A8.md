TITLE: Remove unused anyhow and proptest dependencies from rk-cli
LABELS: enhancement
STATUS: untracked

## Finding
`crates/rk-cli/Cargo.toml` declares `anyhow` (dependency) and `proptest` (dev-dependency). Neither crate is referenced anywhere in the rk-cli sources or tests.

## Evidence
- crates/rk-cli/Cargo.toml:58 `anyhow.workspace = true` and :69 `proptest.workspace = true` @ ce4ed1a and 62dd3b8 (identical).
- `git grep -n "anyhow\|proptest" ce4ed1a -- crates/rk-cli` returns only these two lines, across 469 files under src/ and tests/.
- Cargo.toml:71 and :88: the workspace comment confines `anyhow` to rk-cli and redkiln-migrate (ADR-0102 d6). That is a permission, not a use. No record gives a reason for rk-cli to hold either crate.

## Impact
Cosmetic: a larger dependency graph and slower builds, and a misleading signal that rk-cli uses `anyhow` error plumbing (it uses its own `Halt`/`Exit` types). Low.

## Suggested fix
Delete both lines. Check that `redkiln-migrate` really uses `anyhow` before touching the workspace row. Consider `cargo machete` or `cargo udeps` in CI.

TITLE: Seed the rk-adapter-claude throttle across processes (first dispatch is unguarded)
LABELS: enhancement
STATUS: untracked

## Finding
The rate-limit throttle is process-local. Each `redkiln` process starts with `Guard::empty()`, so its first dispatch skips the headroom check whatever the last journalled `rate_limit_reading` says. Only a code comment records this. No runbook, DECISIONS or ADR record owns it.

## Evidence
- crates/rk-adapter-claude/src/throttle.rs:88-98 @ ce4ed1a: "The first dispatch of a batch is necessarily unguarded".
- crates/rk-adapter-claude/src/lib.rs:205 @ ce4ed1a: `Guard::empty()` at construction.
- Unchanged at 62dd3b8.
- The two neighbouring adapter hardening points are already recorded and are **out of scope** here: the poison `expect`s (ADR-0118, accepted halt) and the unpruned `child-settings/` directory (s8h-cards-and-run-environment.md:1115-1124).

## Impact
The lane is resumed across processes by design (one `redkiln run` per step boundary, kill and resume in Step 8). Every resume therefore starts unguarded and can dispatch into a near-exhausted window. That costs a throttled or failed paid dispatch.

## Suggested fix
At adapter construction, seed the guard from the newest `rate_limit_reading` row in the event store, honouring its `resetsAt`. Add a test that a fresh process with a near-limit reading in the journal refuses or waits on its first dispatch. Optionally, add one sentence to lib.rs:64 carving the ADR-0118 poison halt out of the AC-360 claim.

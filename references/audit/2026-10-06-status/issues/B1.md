TITLE: Build the tier-1 orphaned-anchor and rewrite-event producers (AC-167/169/170/171)
LABELS: bug, enhancement
STATUS: untracked

## Finding
The orphaned-anchor / rewrite-event subsystem of ADR-0018 was declared in types but never built. `Drift::OrphanedAnchor` and `EventKind::Rewrite` are never constructed by production code, and `GitOp::PatchId` has no caller. Step 6 listed AC-160..199 in its acceptance set and closed without AC-167, 169, 170, 171 or 190; no later stage reschedules them.

## Evidence
- crates/rk-types/src/drift.rs:93 @ ce4ed1a: `OrphanedAnchor { anchor, rows }` declared; the only other hits are its name() and an arm in a match (drift.rs:115,136). No construction site in any crate.
- crates/rk-types/src/event_kind.rs:142 @ ce4ed1a: `Rewrite` declared; production hits are only exhaustive-match arms (rk-kernel workflow/breaker.rs:635, fsm.rs:1730, memo.rs:753).
- crates/rk-git/src/git_exec.rs:42,135,175,212 @ ce4ed1a: `GitOp::PatchId` is listed and named but nothing calls it.
- runbook/phase-1/s6-store-and-git.md:478 @ ce4ed1a: "git-as-truth AC-160-AC-199" claimed by Step 6.
- A grep for AC-167/169/170/171/190 across runbook/, spikes/DECISIONS-*.md and references/adr at ce4ed1a finds no stage, owed item or deferral. The only adjacent text is definitional: s6-store-and-git.md:146-147 and ADR-0105 clause 9 place orphan rows and rewrite events outside the AC-184 rebuild differential. PROGRESS.md:622 ("Owed out of Step 6") lists AC-006/008/188/161/164/184 but not these.

## Impact
A rebase, amend or force-push leaves tier-1 rows anchored to unreachable commits with no drift report, no `rewrite` trail and no human gate, which ADR-0018 requires (success measure: every orphan resolves to a rewrite event or an opened gate). Not on the M5 path (the exit runs do not rebase), so this is silent debt rather than a blocker.

## Suggested fix
Schedule it or accept it explicitly. Scheduling: add a stage or s8j/Phase-2 item owning AC-167/169/170/171 and AC-190: have `reconcile` emit `Drift::OrphanedAnchor` for rows whose anchor is unreachable (no deletion); add a patch-id mechanism that writes a `rewrite(old,new,mechanism)` event before any re-anchor; add the AC-170 negative test; open a human gate for AC-171. Accepting: record a dated deferral in PROGRESS.md and drop the claim from s6's acceptance set.

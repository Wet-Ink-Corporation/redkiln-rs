TITLE: Make catalog header loading strict and digest excludes/deviation_ok
LABELS: bug
STATUS: untracked

## Finding
The catalog header loader silently accepts malformed input: a non-array or non-string `provides`/`excludes`/`deviation_ok` yields an empty or partial list, `min_rigor` defaults to "optional", and unknown keys are not rejected. `inputs_digest` hashes eleven hand-picked activity fields and omits `excludes` (read by step 5) and `deviation_ok` (read by step 7).

## Evidence
- crates/rk-kernel/src/catalog.rs:1024-1035 @ ce4ed1a (= 62dd3b8): the `text`/`strings` closures use `filter_map(Value::as_str)` and `unwrap_or_default()`.
- catalog.rs:1335: `min_rigor: text(head, "min_rigor").unwrap_or_else(|| "optional".to_owned())`.
- crates/rk-kernel/src/select/canonical.rs:70-91: `catalog_json` lists id, stage, provides, requires, tags, applicable_if, method_rank, min_rigor, max_rigor, cost_class, family.
- select/step5.rs:522 reads `activity.excludes`; select/step7.rs:190 and :362 read `deviation_ok`.
- Records: runbook/phase-1/s8j-run-preparation.md:905 notes `catalog::walk` dropping a non-string `deviation_ok` entry, but only as a candidate excluded from a survey. No record schedules a fix.

## Impact
A typo in a catalog or pack header changes behaviour silently. A change to excludes or deviation_ok does not move `inputs_digest` or `run_id`, so replay cannot detect it. Low today (the catalog is operator-authored), rising as catalogs become agent-edited.

## Suggested fix
Make `strings` and `text` return `Result` and push a load diagnostic on a wrong type. Reject unknown header keys against a closed set. Require `min_rigor`, or document the default under an ADR. Add `excludes` and `deviation_ok` to `catalog_json`; this moves goldens and the digest, so do it in one dated change.

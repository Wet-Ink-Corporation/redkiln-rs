# Decisions taken for the runbook alignment review, 2026-09-12 (E1…E13)

Same shape as `DECISIONS-2026-09-11.md`. Every entry was taken **by the operator** (Ryan Britton) in a walk-through
of the thirteen decisions the 2026-09-12 runbook alignment review assigned to him, one at a time, with the grounding
and the options in front of him, before any runbook edit that depends on it was made. Grounding is a document
section or a measured number, never taste.

**Numbering.** These carry **`E`-ids**, not the `#1`–`#27` sequence of `DECISIONS-2026-09-11.md`: they come from a
different pass with its own id space, and the runbook cites them as `decision E4`, `decision E9` and so on. Cite
them as `DECISIONS-2026-09-12 E<n>`.

**Where the review came from.** Eleven dimension reviewers over `runbook/`'s nineteen files and the governing
corpus, each finding then put to three independent refuters (source-fidelity, already-dispositioned, materiality);
107 findings raised, 95 survived a 2-of-3 majority. These thirteen are the subset the runbook was **not permitted to
decide for itself** — `00-preamble.md`: *"Where a stage is tempted to decide something else, it stops."*

## Rejected, and why

| # | rejected |
| --- | --- |
| **E1** | *A fourth named domain shape* (`tri_boolean`) — AC-072 closes the vocabulary at three and carries a must-fail fixture for *"a shape none of the three"*, so this breaks an accepted criterion's negative fixture. *Runtime-only, registry untouched* — leaves the engine no declarative basis to expect `null`, which is the "a label, not a gate" shape s4a §4a.1 exists to warn about. |
| **E2** | *An ERRATA entry* — forbidden by ADR-0092 for a `## Decision` sentence. *Narrowing clause 1 instead* (`dispatch` takes `&dyn`) — tidier at the boundary, but moves the signature F41 compiled, AC-497 and design §3.3 P10. *Reading clause 4 as already satisfied* — defensible (the `dyn` inside is bare; `Mutex` is not the user-defined newtype clause 4 forbids) but leaves the next implementer to re-derive it undocumented. |
| **E3** | *Reusing `activity_committed`'s result digest* — cheapest and leaves the closed enum alone, but conflates *"the activity committed"* with *"this artifact was written"*, and one activity may produce several artifacts. *Recording s8 (1b) NOT RUN* — honest, but rests Phase 1's exit on stage-completeness alone. |
| **E4** | *Excluded packages invoked by `--manifest-path`* — green by construction, but needs an erratum against ADR-0100 decision 3's *"scratch member"*, a narrowing of AC-001's *"same invocation"*, and breaks s8 (4)'s `cargo metadata --no-deps` membership check. |
| **E5** | *Pinning a next-action line in the `doctor` rule table* — gives a directly checkable output, but creates a second contract that must stay in step with the canonical serialiser. |
| **E7** | *Install twelve, enable two via `[packs] enabled`* — closer to how the product eventually ships and exercises the enabled-list mechanism, but `doctor` still **loads** all twelve under AC-071/AC-520/AC-072, so ten unreviewed packs would gate Checkpoint 7e's exit 0. |
| **E9** | *N = 20 or N = 10* — faster signal; rejected because the false-positive cost (an operator learning to ignore `doctor`) is unbounded while the latency cost is bounded and small. |
| **E11** | *Reconcile on all five* — maximally safe and keeps principle 4 literally true, but contradicts ADR-0105 clause 8 outright (needing a successor) and puts a reconcile on every roll-up read, against F18's 45–48% of gates exceeding 1 h. *Keeping both as distinct criteria* — leaves the `status` disagreement unresolved in the register, which is the actual question. |
| **E12** | *Phase 4* — most conservative on the gate bar (ADR-0007's E3-shaped checks; S-CODEX unrun), but requires **superseding** an accepted Decision. *Phase-agnostic in the runbook* — nothing in Phase 1 depends on the answer, but it leaves two accepted documents disagreeing in the record. |
| **E13** | *Excluding AC-475 with the rest of the corpus-bound set* — maximal deference to the register, but s5 §5.3's no-synthesis claim would then cite a criterion no Phase-1 stage claims. *Keeping s7d as-is* — leaves nine ids claimed on an instrument the stage cannot run. |

### 1. What the lattice found

**1.1 First run: 23.5%, and three quarters of it was four shapes.** The 6,216 unsatisfiable pre-registered pairs on run1 (`results-p6-run1b-instrument-v3.txt` §"unsatisfiable by") decompose into: (a) *no producer tags this kind* — `ReviewVerdict` for incidents (no review activity tagged `incident`), `Spec` for tech-debt/docs, `ChangeRecord`/`Estimate`/`MigrationLog` for bugs, `SLOReport` for stories, `IncidentTimeline` for a bug postmortem; (b) *a producer's `requires` names a kind whose only producers live in a stage the pack forbids* — `user-story-ac` requires `ProblemStatement`, produced only in discovery, which the story pack forbids: **every story context was unsatisfiable at specification by construction**; `test-after` requires `Spec` for chores (specification forbidden); `appetite-check` requires `SliceSet` for spikes; (c) *a pack row requires a kind at a rigor below the only producer's `min_rigor`* — `Postmortem` at bug closeout `optional`; the whole `optional`-override mode for initiatives (54.8%) is this; (d) *the binary-only Unreal slice* — every implement producer carries `change:text`, so `Changeset`/`ImplReport`/`ChangeSummary` have no producer when `change = {binary}` (41–48% of that slice in every mode). Classes (a)–(c) are tag-, requires- and pack-row-level; (d) is a missing activity class.

**1.2 Two fix rounds took it to 11.4%; the residue is (d) plus four stragglers.** Round 1 (15 edits + `walking-skeleton`, all listed in `catalog/*.toml` comments as "P6 run1 fix" and in `packs/bug-standard.toml`, `packs/project.toml`): 23.5 → 11.5%. Round 2 (`code-review` requires `Spec?`; `schema-migration-script` tags bug): 11.5 → 11.4%. At pack-default the Node slice is now 2.2% (all 12 packs), from four causes: `DataMigrationPlan` for bugs (design optional in the bug pack; `data-migration-plan` does not tag bug), `MigrationTestReport` for bugs (`migration-dry-run` does not tag bug), `Spec` via `adversarial-review` for incidents (specification forbidden), `TestReport` for projects at `criticality = high` (`equivalence-boundary` does not tag project). Each is one tag. The remaining 9.2 points are the binary-only slice.

**1.3 The mixed Node+UE slice: no extra unsatisfiability — and that is the finding.** On a `node+unreal` item (`change = {text, binary}`) the generic `change-summary` (`'text' in change`) and `change-summary-ue` (`'unreal' in toolchain`) are both admissible at rank 10; I5's specificity rule picks the UE one, and the text summary is **never produced**. The report's "specificity wins" table counts it: `change-summary-ue` 448 contexts per bug pack, `unit-tests-ue` 224, `minimal-repro-ue` 448 (`results-p6-run3.txt` §"specificity wins"). PC7 says a story touching Node + UE content "needs both change summaries"; under one artifact kind and one-producer-per-kind selection it gets one, silently. This is exactly the PC7 shape question the task predicted, and it is invisible to the unsatisfiability metric because the DAG is satisfiable — it is just wrong. Reading for the kill: the pre-registered criterion ("any unsatisfiable pair in the mixed slice → reopen PC7") does not fire; the reopening is warranted anyway, on this evidence, and the fix is one of: per-toolchain artifact kinds (`ChangeSummary{toolchain}`), set-valued artifacts, or a `for_each toolchain` fan-out at the pack row.

**1.4 Dead activities: 14 of 15 are the rank rule (F34 B1), one is a pack gap, one is a banding artefact.** Dead in every pack × context: `bdd-outside-in`, `bdd-scenarios`, `bisect`, `canary-release`, `design-sprint`, `failover`, `fault-tree-analysis`, `feature-flag-kill`, `flag-progressive-rollout`, `instrumented-trace-analysis`, `property-based-testing`, `rate-limit-shed`, `rolling-restart`, `use-case-spec` — every one a higher-rank member of an ordered family, admissible in some context but never lowest, i.e. **reachable only by deviation `add`**. `rollback` (release stage) is dead because no pack row requires `RollbackRecord`. `canary-release` is dead for a different reason: it wins only at `blast_radius = med` (rolling takes `< med`, blue-green `>= high`), and the pre-registered banding to {low, high} has no `med` — the lattice cannot reach it (F34 amendment 43). Under the eight pre-registered packs `checklist-review`, `docs-update`, `test-after`, `tech-debt-assessment`, `security-requirements`… join the list (21 / 101, 20.8%) because their kinds (chore, docs, tech-debt, security-vuln) have no pre-registered pack at all; the four supplementary packs (§3, disclosed) bring it to 14.9%. Soft cycles (`design-doc ↔ adr-record`, `design-doc ↔ data-migration-plan`, incident's `hotfix → build-package → rolling-deploy → rollback-mitigation → hotfix`) run only through `?` requires and are the engine's to break by treating optional inputs as non-blocking; no hard cycle exists.

### 3. Amendments to METHOD-P disclosed here (applied by Ryan, not by me)

1. **Eight packs, ten kinds.** §3.2 pre-registers packs for six kinds; chore, tech-debt, security-vuln and docs had none, which makes every activity tagged only for them dead by construction. Four supplementary packs were written (`packs/{chore,tech-debt,security-vuln,docs}.toml`, marked SUPPLEMENTARY) and every metric is reported both ways. The pre-registered reading is the 20.8% dead figure; the supplemented reading is 14.9%.
2. **Four ordinal axes, not five.** `dynamism` is not in `facets.toml`; the lattice is 2⁴ not 2⁵ bands.
3. **Rigor axis as an item override + a pack-default mode.** §3.2 says "3 live rigor levels" without saying how a level combines with the pack's per-stage policy; the override reading (every non-forbidden stage at that rigor) makes `optional` artificially hostile to `min_rigor = recommended` producers (initiative 54.8% on run1). The pack-default mode is what a real item sees; it is the headline.
4. **Instrument-only changes after run0**: failure reasons on transitive `requires`, hard/soft cycle split, the mixed-slice differential, per-toolchain breakdown, `--only-*` filters. Unsatisfiable counts were diffed run0 vs run1b and are identical.
5. **Two fix rounds before the reported figure.** The kill criterion says gaps are filled before dispatch; §1.1's first-run figure is the pre-registration's number, §1.2's is what the pack is at. Both stand.
6. **Results live under `spikes/p9/`** (P9 precedent), not `spikes/results/`.

Interpretations the engine had to fix that the proposal leaves open (I1–I9 in `lattice.py`'s docstring; F34 B1/B2/B8/B19): producers filtered by stage, `requires` from any non-forbidden upstream stage or a `ROOTS` set, `rigor` as an `applicable_if` root, row `rigor` raises the selection rigor, specificity as tie-break, cost points, `exists()` in spawn rows. Change any of I1/I2/I5 and every number above moves.

### 4. Verdict

Against its own criteria P6 kills the v0 pack twice and clears it once: 23.5% (first run) and 11.4% (after two tag-level rounds) are both over the 5% line, so **the pack is not tagged**; the 20.8% dead figure under the pre-registered packs crosses the 20% line by less than one activity; the mixed-toolchain slice adds nothing unsatisfiable. But the decomposition matters more than the totals. Three quarters of the first-run gaps were single-line tag or `requires` mistakes a per-file lint cannot see and a 27-second lattice run finds by name; the residue after two rounds is one deliberately unwritten activity class (a binary-change implement producer) and four tags. The dead list is not fifteen useless activities — it is fourteen higher-rank family members that the "lowest rank wins" rule can only reach by deviation, i.e. **F34 B1 measured**: under PC1 + §2.5 step 4 as written, rigor never escalates a family. And the mixed slice's zero is the wrong kind of zero: on a Node+UE item the engine picks the Unreal change summary and drops the Node one without complaint, which is the PC7 shape defect the spike was extended to catch, showing up as a selection rather than a failure. **Fill the four tags and decide B1/PC7 before Phase 1 writes the selector; run P6 again after; expect < 3% on the text slices and a dead count that reflects the rank decision rather than the catalog.**

### Limits

1. Every artifact `Missing`; `Present`/`Stale` (step 3) and execution are not modelled, so `requires` recursion always selects a producer even where a real item would already hold the artifact.
2. The engine is 300 lines of Python and nine interpretations; the Rust selector will differ on at least I1 and I5, and the numbers with it.
3. Banding to {low, high} hides every `med`-keyed rule; the covering array is pairwise, so three-way boolean interactions are unexplored.
4. Packs are mine, written in one sitting before the first run; appetites are guesses; four of twelve are outside the pre-registration.
5. The catalog author ran the completeness check on his own catalog and chose which gaps to fix between runs; the first-run figure is the one that cannot have been steered.
